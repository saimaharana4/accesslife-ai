# Playing Taste Duel

1. Open [Taste Duel](https://tasteduel-web-902056825143.asia-south1.run.app). Choose Music artist or Film, search for a familiar favourite and select the exact returned entity.
2. Start a duel. Preparation retrieves Qloo candidates and validates a complete five-round path. An unavailable provider or insufficient evidence can prevent a game from starting.
3. Predict which choice Qloo ranked higher for your selected seed in this frozen request. The question is about that ranking, not which item you should like.
4. Read the accepted reveal. “Why this duel?” states the verified ranking context; unsupported psychological or causal explanations are omitted.
5. Choose Next explicitly. After five accepted answers, the server provides your score out of five.

Solo challenge level responds only to prediction performance and the remaining validated pool. It is an engineering heuristic, not AI learning your personality. Answering and advancing do not call Qloo again or change existing winners.

## Discoveries and challenges

Save Discovery is an explicit selection you make. A correct prediction does not save or endorse either contestant. Saved items belong to the anonymous session and remain subject to evidence/session expiry; they are not permanent account bookmarks.

Create a challenge from a completed game and copy its invitation. The invitation exposes the seed, creator’s actual score and expiry. A friend gets a separate guest-owned game with exactly the five delivered comparisons, in the same order and on the same sides. Replay does not adapt or request new rankings. This is casual play, with no global leaderboard or claim of cheat-proof competition.

## Recovery and expiry

Refresh and reconnect restore the authoritative accepted state while the session and evidence remain valid. A delayed response is not permission to count a second answer. Use the displayed retry action rather than creating a different submission after an uncertain response.

Expired or revoked invitations have intentional unavailable pages. They cannot silently become a new challenge or refresh their answers. Clearing the guest session revokes private access and owned live shares under the session policy; it does not rewrite existing backups.

The core flow works with keyboard controls and without motion, audio or a map. Search supports Arrow keys, Enter and Escape. Original title cards are project artwork, not official posters or artist photographs; titles remain readable when images fail. Automated accessibility checks are separate from pending native screen-reader and zoom evaluations.
